# Ruck Load

A Connect IQ data field that adds load-adjusted rucking metrics to watches
without a native ruck profile (fenix 7 series, epix Gen 2),
and works on fenix 8 too.

Garmin's fenix 8 ruck algorithm is not public. This field uses the published
Pandolf (1977) load-carriage equation with the Santee (2003) downhill
correction, so numbers will not match the native feature exactly.

## Metrics

| Label        | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| NO-PACK PACE | Pace that would cost the same energy on flat, paved ground with no pack |
| RUCK CAL     | Load-adjusted calories (grade from barometric altitude, plus terrain); `/H` below is the current burn rate |
| LOAD         | Pack weight x distance, a cumulative work score (unit shown below, e.g. KG-KM) |
| PACK         | Pack weight and % of body weight, with a 10-block energy bar |

### End-of-activity stats (Garmin Connect)

Saved in the activity file and shown in Garmin Connect after syncing (not on
the watch's own summary screen, which Connect IQ fields can't extend):

| Where        | Stat |
|--------------|------|
| Chart        | Ruck metabolic rate (W) over time |
| Summary      | Ruck calories, pack weight (kg), load x distance (kg-km) |
| Summary      | Avg no-pack pace (min/km, while moving), avg ruck burn (kcal/h), peak ruck burn (best 30 s average, after the first minute) |
| Laps table   | Ruck calories per lap |

Connect shows these with fixed labels, so they are always metric, whatever
the weight and distance settings on the watch.

## Look

80s-inspired, with one theme per screen technology:

- **AMOLED (fenix 8, epix): green-phosphor CRT.** Black background,
  dot-matrix font with gaps between the dots (about 30% fewer lit pixels),
  mostly green (the most efficient OLED subpixel), dim labels, outline-only
  empty bar blocks, and a 2 px pixel shift every minute against burn-in.
- **MIP (fenix 7, fenix 8 Solar): LCD handheld.** Follows the activity's
  black or white background with full-contrast ink and solid glyphs; no dim
  tones (they vanish on a reflective screen). Colour only where it matters:
  the pack and the orange/red warning blocks, in pure MIP colours.

Layouts:

- **Full screen:** a walking rucker sprite (it stands still when you stop),
  then no-pack pace, calories | load, and the pack bar.
- **Half screen:** no-pack pace | calories, plus the pack bar.
- **Smaller fields:** the single metric picked in settings.

The bar fills green, then amber from 2/3 of your warning threshold, then red
above it. Past the threshold the red blocks blink.

Battery: nothing animates beyond the normal once-a-second data field
update. The sprite and bar are a few dozen rectangles, and all text is
formatted in `compute()` rather than `onUpdate()`.

Fonts and the icon are generated by `tools/gen_assets.py` (5x7 dot-matrix
glyphs, scaled per screen size). Re-run it after changing glyphs or sizes.

## Setup

1. Build and sideload (see the root README), e.g.
   `monkeyc -o bin/RuckLoad.prg -f monkey.jungle -y ../developer_key.der -d fenix7`
2. On the watch, copy the **Hike** or **Walk** activity, rename it "Ruck", and add
   this data field (full screen recommended). The activity is saved with the
   sport of the profile you copied (hiking or walking); this field can't change
   that. You can change the activity type in Garmin Connect afterwards.
3. Set pack weight, weight unit (kg or lbs), distance unit (km or mi), terrain
   and load warning, either on the watch (during an activity: menu > the Ruck
   Load field's settings) or in the Connect IQ app on your phone. Defaults are
   kg and km. Both edit the same settings, and they are remembered between
   rucks until you change them. Changing kg/lbs on the watch converts your pack
   weight; on the phone, re-enter it.
